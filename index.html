Backups & device setup

Rounds save automatically on this device. Export a backup to keep a copy in Files or move it between your Mac and iPad. Import adds saved rounds without replacing your current ones.

Offline setup requires the hosted HTTPS website.

On iPad: open the hosted site in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Use that same app each time. Safari and Home Screen storage may be separate; use a backup to transfer rounds.

On Mac: use your browser. With Sidecar, move this browser window to your iPad; the Mac still runs and saves the round. Keep the page visible for warnings.
